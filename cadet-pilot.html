Cadet Pilot Program

Elite Pathway

India’s First Cadet Pilot Program with a Global Airline Partner

Powered by Air Prime Asia

Air Prime Asia proudly introduces India’s first-ever Cadet Pilot Program in collaboration with a leading global airline partner, designed to shape the next generation of world class aviators. Built on international training standards and supported by advanced technology, this flagship program offers aspiring pilots a direct, structured, and future-ready pathway from classroom to cockpit.

At Air Prime Asia, our cadets receive comprehensive aviation education, starting from foundational theory to simulator-based performance training and multi-phase flight instruction. With access to experienced airline instructors, state-of-the-art aircraft, and globally recognized safety practices, every student is groomed for real-world airline operations from day one.

This exclusive program ensures seamless progression, combining CPL training, multi-engine experience, airline specific SOP training, type rating readiness, and professional development modules all aligned to global industry requirements. Our partnership with an international airline enables cadets to train, fly, and gain exposure under the same operational culture they will eventually work in, giving them a distinct advantage in pilot recruitment and career growth.

By choosing Air Prime Asia, you join a training ecosystem that values precision, discipline, and excellence. Our mission is not only to train pilots but to empower confident aviation professionals prepared for global opportunities. With India’s first cadet program backed by a global airline partner, your dream of becoming an airline pilot is closer than ever.

The Curriculum

Complete Pathway: CPL + MER + Type Rating + Line Training & Jr First Program (500hrs)

1. Commercial Pilot (CPL)

  • Full ground classes
  • DGCA syllabus coverage
  • Experienced faculty

2. Multi-Engine (MER)

  • Multi-engine training
  • Advanced handling skills
  • High-standard flight safety

3. A320/B737 Type Rating

  • Complete Level-D FFS sim
  • Cockpit systems & profiles
  • Operational SOP training

4. Line Training & Jr First Program (500h)

  • Real airline experience
  • 500 hours on type
  • Start earning in training
