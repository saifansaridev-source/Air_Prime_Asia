Bachelors Degree approved by University of Technology, Jaipur

B.Sc. (Hons) in Aviation Science Degree

"No Profession in the world can be so regulated and constantly reviewed as that of a pilot. Throughout their career they are medically examined, annually trained, checked and evaluated for their professional capabilities in every six months."

Keeping the same in view, a Three Years Bachelors of Science Honors Program in Aviation is introduced along with flying license for improving the professional standards in civil aviation which would result in a healthy industrial and corporate environment.

The Program is a specialized program offered to candidates who are Commercial Pilots or candidates pursuing Pilot Training. The course is approved by the University of Technology, Jaipur, Rajasthan. Candidates already working in the airlines as Pilots, get admission through Lateral Entry in the 2nd year.

Admission Eligibility Schemes

Regular Entry Scheme

  • Admission Semester: 1st Semester
  • Standard: Passed Higher Secondary / 10+2 from a recognized Board
  • Mandatory Core: Physics & Mathematics

Lateral Entry Scheme

  • Admission Semester: 3rd Semester (2nd Year)
  • CPL Holders: Completed or under-issue CPL/CHPL from an ICAO member country
  • DGCA AME: Flight Dispatcher / AME course approved by DGCA, Govt. of India
