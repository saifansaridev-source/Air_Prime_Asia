Develop critical hospitality, safety and emergency management skills

6 Months Certificate Program in Aviation, Hospitality & Tourism

"All over the world, Aviation, Hospitality & Tourism Industry is growing up in a rapid way. Demand of trained and skilled manpower is increasing day by day."

To meet the requirements of the industry, Air Prime Asia has introduced a first-of-its-kind, highly successful model of Technical and Academic collaboration and offers a Six (06) Months Certificate Program in Aviation, Hospitality & Tourism at our head office and through franchise centers to generate a good number of skilled and trained manpower pan India.

Air Prime Asia provides extensive and in-depth training by highly qualified and motivated instructors, who have work experiences in the airline industry. The technical training is conducted on our real wide-body aircraft Airbus A-300, and the entire program is completed without compromising on quality. In a short period of time, our graduates have been hired and are now working with different airline companies locally and across the globe.

The Modules

Our Cabin Crew Training Program Includes

Grooming & Articulation

Includes personality development, professional grooming, communication, mannerisms, and English language proficiency.

Wide-Body Slide Drills

Features hands-on safety equipment training, slide drills, cabin emergency land evacuation, and turbulence procedures.

Fire Safety & Evacuation

Covers smoke drills, fire safety procedures, safety demonstrations, and first-aid checklists.

Inflight Roleplays

Practical customer service training with live role-plays inside aircraft mock-ups, catering and trolley service checks.

Ground Operations

Comprehensive airport management and ground handling training, ticketing, and baggage claim desk basics.

Familiarization Flights

Exposure to active crew coordination and real aircraft familiarization flights between two available sectors.
