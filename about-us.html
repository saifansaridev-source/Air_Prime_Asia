Who We Are

Our 20-Year Aviation Legacy

Air Prime Asia Pvt. Ltd. proudly carries forward a legacy of over 20 years of excellence in aviation, education, and professional services. Our journey began in 2005 with the establishment of Griffin Aviation Academy Pvt. Ltd., one of India's pioneering aviation training institutions specializing in Pilot Training, Cabin Crew Training, Airport Ground Operations, and Aviation Hospitality. The academy earned a strong reputation for delivering industry-oriented education and producing highly skilled aviation professionals for airlines and aviation organizations in India and abroad.

To expand our capabilities, Glorietta Consultants & Services Pvt. Ltd. was established in 2008, offering specialized consultancy in flight operations, aviation management, manpower development, institutional advisory services, and business support. In 2009, our vision extended into higher education with the formation of the Glorietta Society of Education, successfully integrating professional aviation training with academic and university-based programs.

In 2016, Blue Hadal Voyage Pvt. Ltd. further strengthened the group's commitment to education by supporting initiatives in aviation, hospitality, and skill development. Although the company has since concluded its operations, its contributions remain an important part of our journey and continue to inspire our commitment to excellence.

On 2 September 2021, Air Glorietta Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated as the group's unified corporate identity, bringing together decades of expertise, industry leadership, and trusted partnerships under a single brand dedicated to aviation excellence.

A significant milestone was achieved on 19 April 2026 with the launch of Air Prime Asia Pvt. Ltd. and the commencement of our Charter Aviation Services. Beginning with premium charter operations and regional air connectivity, Air Prime Asia is committed to delivering safe, reliable, efficient, and customer-centric aviation solutions while steadily progressing toward the establishment of scheduled airline operations.

Today, Air Prime Asia operates through two strategic business verticals:

Aviation Education – Developing highly skilled pilots, cabin crew, airport professionals, and aviation leaders through world-class training and academic excellence.
Charter Aviation Services – Delivering premium charter flights, regional connectivity, and customized aviation solutions with the highest standards of safety, professionalism, and operational excellence.

Driven by innovation, integrity, safety, and service excellence, Air Prime Asia continues to shape the future of aviation by empowering aviation professionals and connecting communities through reliable air transportation.

Founding Leadership

Anand misra

President & CEO

Anand misra is a distinguished aviation professional, aerospace engineer, educator, and entrepreneur with over 35 years of experience in aviation training, education, business development, and institutional leadership. As President & CEO of Air Prime Asia, he has played a pioneering role in advancing aviation education and skill development in India. A former instructor and administrator at IGRUA, he has successfully established aviation training institutions, developed industry-oriented programs, and fostered international collaborations. His expertise spans aviation education, pilot training, regulatory coordination, and strategic business development, with a continued commitment to developing globally competent aviation professionals.
