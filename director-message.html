The Successful Journey of Our Founder

Anand misra

President & CEO

"A well-qualified post-graduate engineer in Space Engineering & Rocketry having 35 years of distinguished experience in the aviation industry."

A former senior official with the Ministry of Civil Aviation, Government of India, he pioneered Pilot and Cabin Crew Graduation Programs and other aviation education initiatives across the country.

At Air Glorietta, he leads specialized training in Commercial Pilot License (CPL), Type Rating on Airbus – 320 and Boeing – 737, and crew leasing services for global carriers such as Garuda Indonesia. Mr. Mishra also introduced the concept of Base and Line Training Jr First Officer Program support for airlines, helping enhance pilot readiness and reduce training costs while upholding global aviation standards.

A visionary leader and industry pioneer, Mr. Mishra continues to contribute to the development of skilled aviation professionals and the strengthening of India’s presence in the global aviation landscape.

Executive Message

Smita Mishra

Vice-President

"Our greatest reward in establishing Air Prime Asia is to see our students live their dream of flying and experience a different outlook in life, especially when their perspective is beyond the clouds."

Part of Air Prime Asia’s Motivation, as it significantly rose above other aviation training organisations, is to share the excitement of being up in the sky. That is why we have exerted all our efforts to make Air Air Prime Asia: to provide an ideal aviation training, not only for those who dream of flying but also for those who envisioned themselves working in the airline industry.

Air Prime Asia has grown to become one of the best providers of aviation training with the top caliber instructors that have work experiences in the airline industry. In a short period of time, our graduates have been hired and are now working with different airlines companies locally & across the globe. We are the Air Prime Asia, the fastest growing aviation firm in the country.

Shaping the future of aviation education in India with premium training solutions, cadet programs, and specialized university pathways.
