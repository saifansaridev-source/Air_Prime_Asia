Aviation Degree Pathways

"With development of technology civil aviation demands more professionalism in handling airplanes. Responsibility for achieving zero accident rate in airlines rests not only with the pilot but also with all other professionals including those on ground."

Hence proper co-ordination and resource management among men and machines in the overall system becomes more important. The need of the hour is to make all concerned professionals pass through a common platform so that they understand each others needs in a better way and appreciate their responsibilities to provide a healthy and professional civil aviation environment.

Keeping the same in view, Air Prime Asia offers Graduate, Post-graduate and Diploma programs for improving the professional standards in civil aviation, which would result in a healthy industrial and corporate environment.
