Interactive Flight Training

“Great pilots are made, not born. Many possess good eyesight, sensitive hands, and perfect coordination; but the end result is only fashioned by steady coaching, practice, and experience.”

Air Prime Asia operates high-fidelity simulation systems to prepare candidates for active flight deck coordination. By practicing standard SOP checklists and flight maneuvers in a secure virtual environment, trainees learn to manage complex systems and emergency failure checklist variables smoothly before transition to real aircraft.

Simulator Training Modules

Single-Engine Program

Lay the foundation for your aviation journey with our Single-Engine Training Program. This course focuses on aircraft control, flight maneuvers, navigation, and safety — providing the essential skills and confidence required for independent command.

Multi-Engine Program

Advance your career with our Multi-Engine Training Program, crafted to develop mastery in complex flight systems, performance management, and advanced aerodynamics. Trainees gain in-depth knowledge of instrument flying, ensuring competence and precision in all weather and operational conditions.
