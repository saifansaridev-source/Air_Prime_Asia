The Objectives

Objectives of Collaboration

Recruitment Drives

Joint selection drives at Air Prime Asia training campuses and university affiliates across India. Option to invite our trained candidates to your corporate premises.

On-the-Job Training

Structured industrial exposure for ground handling, ramp, cargo, passenger services. Duration: 3-6 months.

Trained Ground Manpower

Ready-to-deploy certified ground manpower trained as per AASSC / NSDC aviation standards. No financial liability for partners.

Crew Leasing & Line hours

Crew Leasing with Garuda - Citilink Indonesia and Line Training Jr First Officer Program with FitsAir Sri Lanka for operational JFO hours.

Joint Certification

Co-branded skill development programs under Skill India and Aerospace & Aviation Sector Skill Council (AASSC) frameworks.

Flexible Operations

Flexible contract structures ensuring operational efficiency, pilot provisioning, and cost optimization.

Shaping the future of aviation education in India with premium training solutions, cadet programs, and specialized university pathways.
