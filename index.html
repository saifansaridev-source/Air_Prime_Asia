The Best Aviation Training Organisation in Country

Air Prime Asia operates a globally recognized training network. Led by aerospace engineer Anand misra, we raise the technical benchmark of training, maintaining flight dispatch networks to strict airworthiness guidelines.

On April 19, 2026, Air Prime Asia was launched, marking a new era in our journey. While embarking on this exciting new venture, Air Prime Asia's well established aviation training programs including pilot training, cabin crew training, airport ground operations, aviation hospitality, and related Program continue uninterrupted, preserving and strengthening our legacy of excellence in aviation education.
