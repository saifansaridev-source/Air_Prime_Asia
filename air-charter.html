Premium Air Charter Services

Air Prime Asia Private Limited

Air Prime Asia is proud to announce its entry into the Indian aviation sector with a vision to provide safe, reliable, and world-class air charter solutions across India and neighboring regions. We are currently in the process of obtaining the Non-Scheduled Operator Permit (NSOP) from the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) and are preparing to commence commercial charter operations in the near future.

With a strong commitment to operational excellence, passenger comfort, and aviation safety, Air Prime Asia aims to become a trusted name in business aviation, medical evacuation, and customized air travel services.

Every aspect of our charter operations is designed to ensure seamless door-to-door coordination, flexible schedules, and private terminal transfers—maximizing comfort and flight efficiency.

Leadership Profiles

Mr. Anand Misra

Aviation Education Pioneer, Aerospace Engineer & Corporate Leader

A distinguished aviation professional and institution builder with more than 33 years of experience across aerospace engineering, aviation education, pilot training and aviation entrepreneurship.

He holds a Master of Engineering in Space Engineering and Rocketry with specialisation in Aerodynamics from Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra, Ranchi, along with Aeronautical Engineering qualifications. His academic and technical foundation supports a safety-led and systems-led approach to aviation business building.

He has served in senior leadership roles including President and Chief Executive Officer of Air Glorietta Pvt. Ltd. and has contributed to aviation training ecosystems, curricula, university aviation programs, industry-academia partnerships and international aviation engagement.

For Air Prime Asia, Mr. Misra adds institutional credibility, training depth, aviation education leadership, regulatory awareness and strategic ecosystem value.

Mr. Arun Kumar Sharma

Aviation Operations, Procurement & Airline Development Leader

A senior aviation professional and entrepreneur with over five decades of professional experience, including more than 31 years in aviation across operations, logistics, procurement and institutional leadership.

He served for approximately 28 years at Indira Gandhi Rashtriya Uran Akademi (IGRUA), one of India's premier aviation training institutions under the Ministry of Civil Aviation, where his responsibilities included administration, logistics, operational management, procurement, policy implementation and aviation resource planning.

He is the Founder Director of Zexus Airways Services Pvt. Ltd. (Zoom Air), contributing to scheduled airline development, regulatory coordination and aviation business management. He has also been associated with DGCA-approved aviation training initiatives.

For Air Prime Asia, Mr. Sharma contributes procurement discipline, aviation resource planning, government-sector institutional exposure, airline development experience and management maturity.

Capt. Rishabh Thakur

Commercial Pilot | DGCA Regulatory Compliance & Aviation Strategy Specialist

DGCA (India) and CAA (New Zealand) licensed Commercial Pilot, type rated on Airbus A320, Boeing 737 NG and P-68 aircraft, with deep expertise across drone training, regulatory documentation and aviation project execution.

He has played a key role in establishing multiple DGCA-approved Remote Pilot Training Organisations (RPTOs), leading end-to-end setup activities including infrastructure development, training operations, documentation, regulatory approvals, inspection readiness and operational launch.

He has also been involved in Non-Scheduled Operator Permit (NSOP) project structuring, contributing to regulatory alignment, business structuring and operational planning.

His expertise includes DGCA documentation, SOP development, SORA preparation, Training Procedure Manuals, compliance frameworks, RPTO setup, NSOP structuring, regulatory liaison, drone training, UAV manufacturing ecosystems, international drone project feasibility, vendor alignment and export-oriented aviation technology initiatives.

His work further extends into the international UAV ecosystem, including drone manufacturing, cargo-drone feasibility, tactical UAV concepts, high-altitude reconnaissance drone projects and aviation technology consulting.

Our Aircraft Assets

Proposed Initial Fleet

As part of our first phase of operations, Air Prime Asia plans to induct a fleet of five aircraft configured for versatile charter profiles:

2x Beechcraft King Air B200

Configured for executive charters, regional connectivity, and corporate travel. Offering comfortable pressurized turboprop flying over short to medium distances.

1x Pilatus PC-12

Configured primarily for Air Ambulance and Medical Evacuation (Medevac) services. Equipped with advanced onboard life-support systems and patient lifts.

2x Bombardier Challenger

Heavy business jets designed for premium domestic and international charter operations. Features standing headroom cabins and intercontinental ranges.

* Future fleet expansion plans include the induction of the Embraer Legacy 600 to cater to long-range executive travel requirements.
