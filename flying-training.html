Training Bases

Flight Training Bases & Fleet

All the Partners of Air Prime Asia in India and abroad have a large fleet of aircraft for imparting flying training as per the requirements of Government of India. Air Prime Asia offers the following programs in flying: Private Pilot Course (PPL), Commercial Pilot Course (CPL), Multi–Engine Endorsement Course (ME), Instrument Rating Course (IR), and Flight Instructor Rating Course (FIR).

Requirements for Issue of PPL

  • Age: Minimum 17 years of age on the date of applying for a license.
  • Education: Minimum qualification required is 10th (PCM) Appeared/Pass.
  • Medical: Class II Medical (Indian) is compulsory medically fit as per DGCA standards.
  • Flying Training: The Candidate has to complete 50 (fifty) hours of flight time.
  • Ground Training: Composite Paper (Regs, Met, Nav) & Technical Specific/General.

Requirements for Issue of CPL

  • Duration: 1 Year | Minimum Age: 18 years on the date of applying.
  • Qualification: 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics.
  • Medical: Class II Medical & Class I Medical by approved DGCA boards.
  • Flying Hours: Evidence of having satisfactorily completed 200 hours flight time.
  • Ground Papers: Air Regulations, Navigation, Meteorology, Engines, RTR practical.
